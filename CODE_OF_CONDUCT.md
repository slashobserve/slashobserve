# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its text is the standard of behavior for everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where they represent it.

## Report a violation

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct report".
The link is https://github.com/slashobserve/slashobserve/security/advisories/new.
Only the maintainers can read the report.
They review it, respond, and keep the reporter's identity private.

Enforcement follows the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, chosen by the impact of the behavior.
